# P1 parity verification (baseline 1.14.4 vs 1.16.5, 1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, 1.20.6, 1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.11)

Method: read-only. Branch sources were extracted with `git archive` into the scratchpad. For each page I compared the implementing classes in three ways.
1. A literal fingerprint (numbers, strings, comparisons, if/for/return) of every class, on every branch, against 1.14.4.
2. Normalised line diffs, with mapping renames folded away, chained 1.14.4 -> 1.16.5 -> 1.18.2 -> 1.19.2 -> 1.20.1 -> 1.20.6 -> 1.21.1 -> 1.21.4 -> 1.21.11.
3. Targeted full reads where a hook, event or vanilla API behaviour matters.

Classes compared: block, tile entity, container, screen, item and message classes of all 29 pages, plus:
- `RedstoneSignalHandler`, `RedstoneSignal`
- the `AsmHandler` redstone and rain hooks
- the transformers (`WorldRedstonePower`, `WorldReaderStrongPower`, `RainShield`, `WorldRenderer`, `GameRenderer`)
- `RandomThings.java` event listeners (chat, contact button/lever, imbue, signal tick, observer notify)
- `MagneticLootModifier`, `ModCapabilities`, `CapUtil`, `ItemHandlerHelper` and the 1.21.11 item-handler adapters
- block loot tables (raw JSON identical on all branches for all 31 blocks)
- registration name sets for blocks, TEs and containers (identical on all branches)

No class is missing on any branch, and every block/TE/container registration is present everywhere. Every `EntityBlock` that ticks has a `getTicker` on 1.18.2 and later. Constants are identical everywhere (80-block rain range, 20-tick pulses, 200-tick brew, 60-tick trigger glass, 100-block room, 50 destabilizer, 9 slots and so on). Redstone output values, durations, GUI clamps and config keys are identical.

## Differences found (10 behavioural, 3 minor or unverifiable)

### D1. Trigger Glass: sand and gravel no longer fall through an open block on 1.16.5 and later. CONFIRMED (both sides read)
- 1.14.4 `block/TriggerGlassBlock.java:72-75` overrides `getMaterial(state)` to return `Material.PLANTS` while `TRIGGERED`.
  - The class comment at :37-42 says this is deliberately how the sand/gravel pass-through is achieved, because vanilla `FallingBlock.canFallThrough` accepts any replaceable material.
- 1.16.5 `TriggerGlassBlock.java:72-77` and every later branch (1.18.2 :72-77, 1.21.11 :71-76) dropped it.
  - 1.16.5 has no per-state material, and only pathing was re-added (`allowsMovement` / `isPathfindable`).
  - The stale javadoc (:38 / :37) still claims the getMaterial trick.
- No coremod or hook touches `FallingBlock` on any branch. Transformer lists are in `src/main/resources/transformer`, and on 1.21.11 `src/transformers/.../RTTransformers.java`. Grep for FallingBlock hits only the Destabilizer.
- Effect: sand or gravel resting on an open Trigger Glass does not start to fall on 1.16.5 and later, because vanilla treats the glass as a solid. A falling entity already in flight still passes through, since the collision shape is empty.
- Wiki fact affected: "Sand and gravel fall through it while it is open".

### D2. Rain Shield: rain particles and rain sounds are only suppressed on 1.16.5 and later. CONFIRMED hook list; effect inferred
- 1.14.4 `transformer/GameRendererTransformer.js:26` patches only `GameRenderer.renderRainSnow` (the falling streaks). The `AsmHandler.getBiomeForRainRender` javadoc at :255-289 and `RainShieldBlock` :28-38 describe nothing else.
  - No hook exists for the rain splash particles and rain sounds.
- 1.16.5 to 1.21.1 `WorldRendererTransformer.js:26-27` patch both `renderRainSnow` / `renderSnowAndRain` and `addRainParticles` / `tickRain` (the sound/splash method).
  - 1.21.4 and 1.21.11 patch `WeatherEffectRenderer.getPrecipitationAt` (`WorldRendererTransformer.js:17-21`; `RTClientTransformers.java` WorldRenderer), which serves both render and particles.
- Effect: on 1.14.4 splash particles and rain sounds keep playing inside the shield's range.
- Wiki fact affected: "Rain particles and rain sounds are also suppressed in its range".
- The server-side part (`Level.isRainingAt`, 80 blocks, `AsmHandler.overrideIsRainingAt`) is identical everywhere.

### D3. Imbue on-hit effects: a different damage event from 1.21.1. SUSPECTED
- 1.14.4 `RandomThings.java:204`, 1.19.2 :216, 1.20.1 :214 and 1.20.6 :219 use `LivingHurtEvent`.
- 1.21.1 :219, 1.21.4 :220 and 1.21.11 :220 use `LivingIncomingDamageEvent`.
- `LivingIncomingDamageEvent` fires earlier in the damage pipeline, before invulnerability-frame, shield and absorption handling. A hit that vanilla would discard, such as one within the target's i-frames or one blocked by a shield, can therefore still ignite or poison the target on 1.21.1 and later. On 1.14.4 to 1.20.6 it cannot.
- Wiki facts affected: Fire/Poison/Wither imbue "on hit". Timing and conditions are not stated on the page.
- The Experience imbue (`LivingExperienceDropEvent`) is unchanged.

### D3b. Imbue "direct melee hit" filter differs. SUSPECTED, edge case
- 1.14.4 :201-211 to 1.19.2: damage source must be an `EntityDamageSource` and not an `IndirectEntityDamageSource`, and `getTrueSource()` must be living.
- 1.20.1 and later: `getEntity() != null && getDirectEntity() == getEntity()`.
- A player-caused explosion (`explosion.player` on 1.14.4) and similar sources are counted on 1.14.4 to 1.19.2 but not on 1.20.1 and later. Plain melee, arrows and potions behave the same.

### D4. Advanced Redstone Repeater and Torch: "already scheduled" test changed on 1.18.2 and later. SUSPECTED, low impact
- 1.14.4 and 1.16.5 use `getPendingBlockTicks().isTickPending(pos, this)`:
  - `AdvancedRedstoneRepeaterBlock.java:97` (1.14.4), :99 (1.16.5)
  - `AdvancedRedstoneTorchBlock.java:178` (1.14.4), :181 (1.16.5)
- 1.18.2 and later use `getBlockTicks().willTickThisTick(pos, this)`:
  - `AdvancedRedstoneRepeaterBlock.java:94` (1.18.2), :93 (1.21.11)
  - `AdvancedRedstoneTorchBlock.java:176` (1.18.2), :174 (1.21.11)
- This mirrors vanilla's own change. `willTickThisTick` is true only for a tick due this very tick, while `isTickPending` is true for any queued tick.
- Effect: when the input toggles again while a long on/off delay is still pending (up to 120 ticks), 1.18.2 and later queue an extra tick and 1.14.4 / 1.16.5 do not. This can cause an extra delayed flip in rare fast-toggle cases.
- Plain delays (0-120, default 20), torch 0-15 strengths and the 8-toggles-per-60-ticks burnout rule are the same code on every branch.

### D5. Dyeing Machine: the "remove dye" result slot is shown for fewer stacks from 1.20.6. SUSPECTED, cosmetic
- 1.14.4 `container/DyeingMachineContainer.java:94`: with an item in the left slot and no dye, the result is a copy whenever `copy.getTag() != null`. That is true for any NBT, including enchantments and damage.
- 1.20.6 :86 and later: the condition is `StackTag.get(copy) != null`, which is only the custom_data component. Enchantments and damage are separate components, so a plain enchanted tool offers no result.
- Dyed items (`rtDye`, `enchantmentColor`) still get the clean-up result everywhere. `StackTag.set` removes the empty component, so stacking is restored.
- The 1.14.4 behaviour for plain enchanted or damaged items (a no-op copy) is arguably a quirk.

### D6. Biome Radar: search samples 3D biomes from 1.18.2. Informational (inherent to the vanilla change)
- 1.14.4 `tileentity/BiomeRadarTileEntity.java:85` uses `world.getBiome(testPos)`, a 2D column lookup. 1.16.5 does the same.
- 1.18.2 :84 and later use `level.getBiome(testPos).value()`, a 3D lookup at the radar's own Y. Cave biomes are only found if the radar is at that depth.
- The wiki's "at its height" is only true from 1.18.2 on.
- Spiral, 5 samples per tick, 60-tick validity poll and the finish/paper logic are identical.
- 1.16.5 to 1.20.6 compare the found biome by registry name (a fix for data-pack biomes), while 1.14.4 compares by instance. This is harmless.

### D7. Block Destabilizer: minor mechanics. SUSPECTED, low
- 1.14.4 and 1.16.5 (`BlockDestabilizerTileEntity.java:236`) add `new FallingBlockEntity(...)` with the original block still in place. The entity removes the block on its first tick.
- 1.18.2 and later (:234) use `FallingBlockEntity.fall(level, pos, state)`, which swaps the block for air at once. This is a one-tick timing difference.
- The "full cube" test is `isNormalCube` (1.14.4 :135, :186) versus `isRedstoneConductor` (1.18.2 :133, :184). The two vanilla predicates differ for a few edge-case blocks.
- Limit 50 (config), lazy and fuzzy mode, sorting and "skip tile entities" are the same.

### D8. Player Interface: armor-slot validation uses a different slot resolver. SUSPECTED, low
- 1.14.4 :166 (to 1.20.6 :162) use `Mob.getEquipmentSlotForItem(stack)`.
- 1.21.1 :162 and 1.21.11 :174 use `stack.getEquipmentSlot()`, with an `Equipable` fallback on 1.21.1 only.
- Item classes that count as armor can differ (carved pumpkin, skulls, equippable-component items). Side mapping (top 36-40 armor, bottom 0-9, north 40-41, others 9-36) is identical.

### D9. Potion Vaporizer fuel lookup. SUSPECTED, low
- 1.14.4 :146 (to 1.21.1 :141) read the vanilla furnace fuel map directly.
- 1.21.4 :141 and later ask `server.fuelValues().burnDuration(stack)` through `FuelHelper`, with a client-side copy fallback. The slot test in `PotionVaporizerItemHandler` is also `burnTime > 0` at :37.
- Fuel can differ if a data pack or mod changes fuel values.
- Potion effects (first effect only, non-instant, 80 ticks per refresh, 205 for night vision), the 100-block room test and the collision-shape rule are identical.

### Not verifiable by reading (risk notes, no confirmed difference)
- **1.21.11 contents drop on break.** Several blocks (Advanced Redstone Interface, Iron Dropper, Item Collector, Advanced Item Collector, Entity Detector, Global Chat Detector, Notification Interface, Block Breaker, Biome Radar) now run their old `onRemove` body (`rtOnRemove`) from `BlockEntity.preRemoveSideEffects`.
  - It is called as `block.rtOnRemove(state, level, pos, level.getBlockState(pos), false)`, and the body is guarded by `state.getBlock() != newState.getBlock()`.
  - Whether the position already holds the new block at that moment depends on the vanilla call order, which I could not read. If it does not, inserted position filters or items would not drop on 1.21.11.
  - No 1.21.11 test covers it, and `TESTING_CHECKLIST.md` rows 77/82/83/87 are blank.
  - Example: `tileentity/redstoneinterface/AdvancedRedstoneInterfaceTileEntity.java` preRemoveSideEffects, with the guard in `AdvancedRedstoneInterfaceBlock.rtOnRemove`.
- **1.21.11 target-inventory adapter.** `itemhandler/ResourceHandlerItemAdapter.java:82-85` reports `getSlotLimit` as a constant 64.
  - Inventory Tester, Block Breaker, Item Collectors and Iron Dropper insert into non-mod inventories through it (`CapUtil.itemHandler`, 1.21.11).
  - The `ItemHandlerHelper.insertItemStacked` port is equivalent to Forge's for stackable and non-stackable items.
- **Redstone power coremods.** The hooks wrap getSignal and getDirectSignal on every branch (`Level.getSignal` on 1.18.2/1.19.2, `SignalGetter` from 1.20.1). The 1.14.4 to 1.21.4 JS transformers patch only the first `IRETURN`, and 1.21.11 patches all returns.
  - I could not check the real bytecode shape. The vanilla method is a single return via a ternary, as far as I recall.
  - `AsmHandler.overrideRedstonePower` / `overrideStrongPower` logic is identical on all branches.

## Asides (identical on all branches, not parity bugs; for the wiki-check phase)
- **Redstone Tool linking.** `BasicRedstoneInterfaceBlock.use` and `RedstoneObserverBlock.use` open the GUI for any held item and return SUCCESS.
  - Vanilla only calls the item's `useOn` when the block interaction is skipped, i.e. while sneaking.
  - The wiki's plain "right click the interface, then the target" probably only works with shift held. The tool's own `useOn` links correctly when reached, and the tests call it directly.
- **Online Detector name case.** `PlayerList.getPlayerByUsername` / `getPlayerByName` is case-insensitive in vanilla on all these versions. The wiki says the player name is case sensitive.
- **Rain Shield "no snow forming".** `Level.isRainingAt` is the only server hook on every branch. Vanilla snow accumulation does not go through `isRainingAt` as far as I can tell from the hooks, so the snow claim may not hold anywhere.
