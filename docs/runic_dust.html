Runic Dust

Item

Description

Runic Dust is a decorative item can be placed on top of other blocks. There is a colored version for each vanilla dye. Right click the top of a solid block to place dust: the block is split into a 4x4 grid and each right click puts one dust into the cell you clicked, using up one dust. Together the dust forms a rune. Right clicking a placed rune with a piece of paper will create a Runic Pattern holding that rune's pattern (one paper is used up) which you can then use to easily copy patterns onto other blocks. Right clicking the top of a solid block with a Runic Pattern places a copy of the rune and uses up one matching dust per cell from your inventory (cells without dust stay empty, creative mode needs none). Sneak and right click with a Runic Pattern to turn it back into paper. Left clicking a rune removes all of it and drops its dust.

Properties

Obtain Crafted from clay, Luminous Powder and a dye (see recipe)
Colors 16, one for each vanilla dye
Grid 4x4 cells per block, one dust per cell
Config FlatRunes (visual), default false - replaces the noisy texture with a flat one

Recipe

Clay Ball
Luminous Powder
Any Dye
Runic Dust ×8

Shapeless: the ingredients can be placed anywhere in the crafting grid. The dye decides the color; all 16 dye colors work.
